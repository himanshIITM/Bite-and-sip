About Bite & Sip

A Kitchen Run Like a Family Table

Tucked right at Pandeypur Chauraha, Bite & Sip keeps things simple: a fully vegetarian kitchen, a calm air-conditioned room, and food that's cooked to be eaten slowly, not rushed. Diners keep telling us the same thing — it's the kind of place you come back to, not just visit once.

No alcohol, no loud music, no fuss — just North Indian comfort food, a few Chinese favourites, easy mocktails, and a team that remembers regulars by name.

  • Dine-in, takeaway & delivery
  • Birthday & anniversary bookings
  • Formal, family-friendly seating
  • Open all seven days
